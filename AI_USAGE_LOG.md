# AI Usage Log

I used AI throughout this project as a technical support tool. It helped me review and improve code, understand the COCO annotation structure, inspect the dataset, and troubleshoot unexpected issues during preprocessing.

For example, AI helped me compare the original bounding boxes with the segmentation polygons, understand how to recalculate boxes from polygon coordinates, and review the conversion to YOLO format. It also helped me identify possible problems with multi-polygon annotations, duplicate labels, invalid coordinates, and image-label consistency.

I also used AI to discuss how the original food categories could be grouped into 10 broader classes, review the model evaluation metrics, analyze prediction errors, and organize the final notebook and repository.

I was responsible for choosing the project direction, deciding which suggestions to use, running the code, reviewing the outputs, testing the model, and making the final decisions about the dataset, classes, filtering process, training setup, and conclusions.
